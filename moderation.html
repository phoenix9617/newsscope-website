UGC Moderation & Community Guidelines

Last Updated: November 10, 2026 • Effective Date: November 10, 2026

Platform: NewsScope (https://newsscope.services)
Operator: New Reach Venture

NewsScope is built to foster thoughtful, balanced, and civil public discourse around global news and current events. To ensure a safe, reliable, and constructive environment for all users, we enforce a strict, zero-tolerance approach toward abuse, harassment, and harmful content.

By submitting comments, participating in discussions, voting, or otherwise interacting with user-generated features within the NewsScope mobile application or web services, you agree to comply with these Guidelines.

1. Zero-Tolerance Policy for Abusive Content

NewsScope maintains an absolute zero-tolerance policy for egregious violations. Any user account found engaging in, promoting, or distributing the following categories of content will face immediate, permanent account termination and content removal without prior warning:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) & Exploitation: Any material that depicts, promotes, or facilitates the sexual abuse or exploitation of minors. All occurrences are immediately purged and reported to legal authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).
  • Hate Speech & Discrimination: Content that attacks, demeans, incites violence against, or dehumanizes individuals or groups based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, disability, age, sex, gender identity, or sexual orientation.
  • Terrorism, Violent Extremism & Threat of Harm: Statements advocating violence, glorifying terrorist organizations, coordinating physical attacks, or expressing specific threats to harm individuals, public safety, or critical infrastructure.
  • Non-Consensual Intimate Imagery (NCII) & Severe Harassment: Targeted bullying, stalking, dox activities (releasing private phone numbers, home addresses, or personal documents), or sharing explicit imagery without clear consent.
  • Self-Harm & Suicide Encouragement: Content encouraging, instructing, or glorifying suicide, self-injury, or eating disorders.

2. Prohibited Content & Community Standards

To preserve the utility and integrity of news discussions, the following behaviors and content types are strictly prohibited:

  • Spam & Commercial Solicitation: Posting automated promotional links, affiliate schemes, repetitive multi-thread comments, phishing URLs, or malware distribution.
  • Coordinated Inauthentic Behavior: Operating automated bot networks, artificial engagement rings, coordinated downvoting/upvoting campaigns, or impersonating public figures, journalists, or other users.
  • Deliberate Deceptive Manipulation: Fabricating quotes, altering headlines maliciously to mislead, or posting deceptive deepfakes presented as authentic factual reporting.
  • Profanity & Vulgarity in Public Discussions: Gratuitous vulgarity, obscene slurs, or aggressive personal insults directed toward community members or contributing journalists.

3. Built-In User Protection & Blocking Capabilities

We provide users with direct, immediate control over their experience in the NewsScope app:

  • User Blocking:
    • You can block any user directly from their profile card or by tapping the three-dot action menu (⋮) on any comment they post.
    • Once blocked, the restricted user's comments, discussions, and profile will be instantly hidden from your view across the application.
    • Blocked users are permanently prevented from replying to your comments, mentioning your username, or interacting with your profile.
  • Managing Blocked Accounts: You can view, manage, and unblock accounts at any time via Profile > Settings > Privacy & Safety > Blocked Accounts.

4. In-App Reporting Mechanisms

NewsScope empowers every user to flag inappropriate or policy-violating contributions quickly and discreetly:

  1. How to Report a Comment or Post:
    • Tap the Menu / Options (⋮) icon located next to the specific comment or discussion item.
    • Select Report Content.
    • Choose the primary violation category (e.g., Harassment / Bullying, Hate Speech, Spam / Bot, Misinformation, or Violence / Illegal Acts).
    • Submit the report.
  2. How to Report a User Profile:
    • Open the user's public profile within the app.
    • Tap Report Profile from the menu and specify the reason.
  3. External / Guest Reporting:
    • If you encounter objectionable content and do not have an active account or app access, you can email our moderation desk directly at sean@newsscope.services with links or screenshots of the material.

5. Content Moderation & Enforcement Workflow

NewsScope employs a hybrid moderation architecture combining automated filtering with human administrative review:

  • Automated Screening: Incoming user text passes through automated safety filters that detect known offensive terms, spam links, and threatening syntax before or immediately after posting.
  • Human Review SLA: All user-flagged items enter a prioritized administrative moderation queue. Reports are reviewed by human moderators within 24 hours.
  • Actionable Enforcement Measures:
    • Content Removal: Deletion or permanent obscuring of offending comments.
    • Temporary Account Suspension: Restricting posting, voting, and commenting privileges for minor or first-time offenses (e.g., 24 hours to 7 days).
    • Permanent Account Banning: Full revocation of user account access, authentication tokens, and blacklisting of associated identifiers for severe or repeat violations.
    • Device & IP Level Restriction: In cases of severe abuse or coordinated spam operations, underlying network access identifiers may be blocked at our firewall.

6. Appeals & Contact

If you believe your content or account was moderated or suspended in error, you may submit an appeal:

  • Email: sean@newsscope.services
  • Subject Line: "Moderation Appeal - [Your Username]"
  • Required Details: Registered account email, approximate date/time of the action, and a concise explanation.

Appeals are reviewed by a senior administrator within 3 to 5 business days, and the decision will be communicated via email.
